Manual-mode notes

Which page lands in which slot?

Reading order: the app numbers your boxes top row first, left to right — slot ① takes page 1, ② takes page 2, and so on, sheet after sheet. Move a box and its number follows the new position.

Does flipping portrait / landscape erase my layout?

No. Your boxes are stored as sheet-relative positions, so flipping the sheet keeps every box exactly where you put it — they just land on the rotated page.

My pages aren't the same size — will they stretch?

Never. Each page is fitted inside its slot keeping its own aspect ratio, centred with white margins. Nothing is cropped or squashed.
