Three steps to a clean copy

1

Cover. Draw a rect, ellipse, brush stroke, or blur box over the stamp. The dropper samples the exact paper colour so patches disappear.

2

Multiply. Stamps sit in the same spot on every page — one click applies page-1 covers to the whole file. Blur forgives small drifts.

3

Download. Covers are baked into a new *-rapper.pdf. Shape covers stay vector, so text under them stays selectable; only blur pages get re-rendered.

Cover tips
  • Blur boxes forgive small stamp drift across pages — make them a little bigger than the stamp.
  • Shape covers stay vector: text under them stays selectable in the exported PDF.
  • Only pages with blur regions get re-rendered — everything else is byte-identical to your original.
